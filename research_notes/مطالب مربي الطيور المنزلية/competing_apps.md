# Competing poultry / backyard-flock apps and how they handle reminders and alerts

Research date: 2026-11-13. Sources are store listings, aggregators (appgoblin, mwm.ai), vendor pages and forum threads. Google Play listing pages did not render for the fetch tool (only title and navigation came back), so Play data below comes from search snippets or from appgoblin. Store listings are marketing copy. Review content is treated as evidence of demand only.

## 1. Which apps exist for backyard keepers and small farms (install counts, ratings)?

### Takeaway
The market is crowded with small, recent apps, mostly iOS backyard trackers from 2025–2026 with almost no ratings. Android has a few larger "farm manager" apps from Pakistan and East Africa (about 150K installs at most). Arabic apps are mostly marketplaces, guides or ornamental-bird breeding tools. No Arabic backyard app with a strong reminder system turned up.

### Cited Findings
**Global, backyard-oriented (mostly iOS, 2025–2026):**
- Backyard Chicken Keeper – Coop (iOS, free): daily egg counter, per-bird production tracking, health events with follow-up reminders. Per its listing, logging a daily count takes under 10 seconds. Detailed mode tracks individual hens and egg colours, plus expenses — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6760012302)
- Backyarder: Chicken Tracker (iOS): $9.99 one-time, no ads or subscription, data stays on the device, works offline, daily reminder to log eggs — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6755912571); [mwm.ai](https://mwm.ai/apps/backyarder-chicken-tracker/6755912571)
- FlockFriend: Chicken Tracker (iOS, No Worries! Lifestyle LLC): free, Pro $9.99/month or $39.99/year. 4.5★ from only 2 ratings. Version 3.7.3 (Sep 16). Egg log, hen profiles, charts, iCloud sync — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6745474579)
- Henstead (iOS/iPad): free, with a one-time Premium (₺499.99 on the Turkish store) for unlimited hens and coops, sales, financial reports and export. Too few ratings to show. Versions 1.3.0 (16 Mar) to 1.4.9 (1 Oct), English only — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/tr/app/henstead/id6759896256)
- CotCot – My Chicken Coop (iOS, French origin): released June 15, 2026, version 1.1. 4.3/5 from 3 ratings. Free tier allows 2 hens and 7 days of history. Premium adds unlimited hens, profitability tools, exports and AI actions. The FAQ says there is no subscription and offers credit packs instead — [mwm.ai](https://mwm.ai/apps/cotcot-mon-poulailler/6778824979)
- NestKeeper (iOS, free): built-in reminder system — [search snippet via App Store results](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6753740122)
- Roost: Backyard Chickens (iOS): free with in-app purchases, works offline, no account needed — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/roost-backyard-chickens/id6761755516)
- Flockstar: the app forum users recommend most often. It tracks sales, expenses, hatches, eggs (weight, damaged) and individual birds. Pricing is unclear: one forum user calls it free, the App Store says it needs a subscription, and another user mentions about $30/year — [BYC thread](https://www.backyardchickens.com/threads/best-apps-for-managing-flock.1335556); [BYC thread](https://www.backyardchickens.com/threads/is-there-an-app-or-something-to-track-egg-laying.1442484/page-2)
- Forum users also mention FlockPlenty, Count Your Eggs and Mother Hen (a cheap one-time purchase, used for a breeding flock) — [BYC threads](https://www.backyardchickens.com/threads/is-there-an-app-or-something-to-track-egg-laying.1442484/latest)
- HenWise – Chicken Coop Weather Guide (iOS): a weather and welfare app, covered in section 3 — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/henwise/id6762583691)

**Global, small-farm "manager" apps (Android-heavy):**
- Easy Poultry & Chicken Manager (com.zaheer.poultry, Zaheer Udeen, Pakistan): 147K+ installs, 3.97★ from 304 ratings, updated Jun 3, 2026. Covers flocks and batches, mortality, eggs and sales, feed stock, health/vaccination/medicine records, finance, multiple users with roles, PDF reports, Google Drive backup. Supports chickens, ducks, turkeys, quail and peacocks. - EggPulse: Smart Poultry Farm (Android): advertises vaccination, treatment and health reminders, and covers turkeys, ducks and geese — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.eggpulse.app) (from a search snippet; the page did not render)
- Other Play/iOS farm managers that came up: My Poultry Manager (and.drew.nkhukumanagement; "nkhuku" means chicken in Chichewa) — [appgoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/and.drew.nkhukumanagement); Layer Farm – PoultryPro+ — [appgoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/com.poultryProPlus); Poultry Farm Management App (Njoro Tech, Kenya) with health and vaccination tracking — [appgoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/com.njorotech.poultryfarmmanagementapp); FlockFarm: Poultry Manager with Google Drive backup — [appgoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/com.flockfarms.poultrymanager); Turuke (vaccination logging) — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.turuke.app)

- Tokuku: poultry marketplace and farm tool that says it sends automated vaccination and treatment reminders. The channel is not stated — [tokuku](https://tokuku-web.web.app/about)

Version 1.1.5 improved pair management. **Its name and package collide with this project's old name "مُربّي"** — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.morbi.manager&hl=en_US) (search snippet)
- "إدارة الطيور" (com.wwr.birds): Arabic app for bird breeders covering poultry, falcons, pigeons, parrots and ornamental birds — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.wwr.birds&hl=en_US) (snippet)
- "عالم الدواجن" (app3527158.avn): built by an individual keeper. Feed calculations, health tracking, production records, and alerts for vaccination dates, feeding and periodic weighing — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=app3527158.avn&hl=en_AU) (snippet only)
- "داجن" (com.dagen): farm management, cycle tracking, market prices, and a guide with feeding and vaccination programmes — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.dagen&hl=en) (snippet)

### Inferences
- The backyard segment is fragmented. Nearly every iOS app was launched in 2025–2026 and has fewer than 5 ratings, so no player dominates. Arabic speakers have no reminder-focused backyard app; Arabic poultry apps are commercial broiler tools, marketplaces, or ornamental/pigeon breeding apps.
- Possible branding problem: "مربي" is already a Google Play app name (com.morbi.manager), which supports the v2.0 rename to "طيور في بيتي".

### Gaps
- Install counts and ratings for the Arabic Play apps, EggPulse and Turuke could not be retrieved, because the Play pages did not render.
- I did not find "مزرعتي" as a poultry app.
- Hobby Farms' "5 Poultry Apps" and Animal Health Digest's app roundup were found but not read — [Hobby Farms](https://www.hobbyfarms.com/?p=75167); [Animal Health Digest](https://animalhealthdigest.com/apps-to-help-manage-backyard-chickens/)

## 2. Reminder and alert types, scheduling, push while closed, snooze/done, per-flock

### Takeaway
Most backyard apps offer generic recurring task reminders (collect eggs, clean the coop, health check, log eggs) and stock-low alerts. Only a few advertise treatment and withdrawal tracking (CotCot) or vaccination reminders (EggPulse, Tokuku, Farmcore, عالم الدواجن). None of the listings found says it auto-generates vaccinations from flock age (day 7/14/21). Listings rarely describe snooze, or whether the schedule is time-of-day or every N hours.

### Cited Findings
- FlockFriend sends "nudges" to collect eggs, clean the coop and schedule health checks. Recurrence, snooze and marking done are not described — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6745474579)
- Henstead offers "fully customizable" reminders for egg collection, health checks, tasks and inventory alerts, plus recurring chores with completion tracking. Interval and time options are not described — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/tr/app/henstead/id6759896256)
- NestKeeper reminders have a title, date, frequency and status tracking — [search snippet](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6753740122)
- Backyarder offers a daily reminder to log eggs — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6755912571)
- Coop attaches follow-up reminders to health events — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6760012302)
- CotCot has recurring tasks for red mites, worming, beak and claws, disinfection, vaccination, nest-box cleaning and litter changes. It tracks treatment plans and egg withdrawal periods after a treatment, and its alerts cover laying drops, weight loss, heat, avian-flu level, and feed or bedding running out — [mwm.ai](https://mwm.ai/apps/cotcot-mon-poulailler/6778824979)
- Easy Poultry Manager offers low-stock alerts. Its listing does not mention reminders, though it keeps vaccination and medicine records — [appgoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/com.zaheer.poultry)
- EggPulse advertises vaccination, treatment and health reminders — [Google Play snippet](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.eggpulse.app)
- عالم الدواجن advertises alerts for vaccination dates, feeding and periodic weighing — [Google Play snippet](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=app3527158.avn&hl=en_AU)
- Farmcore delivers vaccination due-date notifications in-app, by push, or by SMS (SMS on the paid tier only) — [Farmcore](https://farmcore-byj.pages.dev/)
- Tokuku advertises automated vaccination and treatment reminders — [tokuku](https://tokuku-web.web.app/about)
- تفقيس البيض بلس lets the user build an incubation plan per bird type and pick daily reminders — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/%EC%95%8C-%EB%B6%80%ED%99%94-%EA%B4%80%EB%A6%AC%EC%9E%90/id1637798439?l=ar)
- A FarmstandApp article says the best apps send push notifications for upcoming vaccinations — [FarmstandApp](https://www.farmstandapp.com/?p=115034)
- Several app vendors' guides blame missed Android notifications mainly on device battery-saving restrictions, which differ by manufacturer — [Ubeya](https://learn.ubeya.com/en/articles/6556921-troubleshoot-notifications-not-showing-on-android-crew-app); [Daylio](https://daylio.net/faq/android-notifications-are-not-working); [Tasks.org](https://www.tasks.org/docs/troubleshooting_notifications)

### Inferences
- Gaps nobody seems to fill: an age-based schedule generated from the flock's start date; a withdrawal countdown that blocks "eat or sell eggs/meat" (only CotCot comes close); reminders for lighting and every-N-hours water/feed; and Arabic text. These look like a differentiation opportunity.
- Android notification reliability (Doze and OEM battery killers) is a known industry-wide failure mode. A native scheduled-notification layer, which this project has in `src/platform/native.ts`, plus in-app guidance on battery settings, deals with it directly.

### Gaps
- No listing found documents snooze, every-N-hours scheduling, or per-flock versus global scoping. This would need hands-on testing.
- I found no user reviews that specifically complain about reminders not firing in a poultry app. Play reviews were not retrievable, and the search for "Poultry Manager notifications not working" returned only non-poultry results.

## 3. Weather and heat-stress alerts, IoT sensors, behaviour/activity tracking

### Takeaway
HenWise is the only consumer backyard app found that alerts on weather (heat, frostbite, storms). CotCot lists a "heat" alert. Sensor monitoring is otherwise done with general Wi-Fi/cellular thermometers (Govee, UbiBot, Sensaphone) or farm-scale IoT. No backyard app found tracks behaviour or activity.

### Cited Findings
- HenWise (iOS, English only, free with Pro at $4.99/month after a 7-day trial; ₹499 on the Indian store): daily welfare score (SAFE/MILD/WARNING/CRITICAL) for heat or cold, from local weather (Open-Meteo), breed, comb type, age, feathering and coop ventilation. Includes a daily action checklist (check water, ventilation, vulnerable birds first) and a risk breakdown (temperature, humidity, dew point, wind). Pro adds a 7-day forecast, 15 US breeds, multiple flocks, a lowest-risk inspection window, 90-day history, and push alerts for heat waves, frostbite risk and storms. No account needed; data stays on the device — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/henwise/id6762583691)
- CotCot lists a heat alert and an avian-flu-level alert — [mwm.ai](https://mwm.ai/apps/cotcot-mon-poulailler/6778824979)
- University of Guelph and OMAFRA released a free app that rates livestock and poultry heat stress from "No Heat Stress" to "Emergency" — [Farms.com](https://farms.com/ag-industry-news/mobile-app-calculates-heat-stress-in-livestock-poultry-720.aspx); [Poultry World](https://poultryworld.net/home/new-app-to-evaluate-heat-stress-in-poultry-farms)
- Coop sensor reviews describe high/low threshold alerts on the phone (Govee, for example) and say text alerts can be more reliable than app pushes. They advise alerts for lost connection and low battery, and warn that a sensor in direct sun reads falsely high — [FarmstandApp](https://www.farmstandapp.com/317235/5-best-wireless-temperature-alerts-for-small-farms/)
- Kuku Smart ships a "Smart Brooder" device that monitors coop conditions — [Razor Informatics](https://razorinformatics.co.ke/portfolio/chicken-farming-with-kuku-smart)
- A DIY Arduino coop monitor sends temperature and humidity over a cellular SIM — [Soracom](https://soracom.io/smart-ag-through-iot-chicken-coops/); there is also commercial IoT barn monitoring — [HARDWARIO/Schaumann](https://www.hardwario.com/case-studies/schaumann-cz---efficient-monitoring-of-the-stable-environment-using-iot-by-hardwario); [UbiBot](https://ubibot.com/news/4409/chicken-farm-temperature-and-humidity-monitoring)
- Hakli (Arabic) monitors the field environment from connected sensor data — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/bb/app/hakli/id6753976619)

### Inferences
- A weather-driven heat alert that needs no sensor, works offline after the forecast is fetched, and is in Arabic would be new for Arabic speakers. HenWise confirms there is demand and a model people pay for (heat checklist plus push), but it is English-only, US-focused, and subscription-based.
- "Activity/behaviour" monitoring appears only in commercial or AI-vision contexts. For backyard keepers it would realistically mean manual symptom or behaviour logging, not sensing.

### Gaps
- No ratings or reviews exist yet for HenWise, so actual demand can't be measured.
- No backyard app found does behaviour or activity tracking.

## 4. What users praise, complain about, or request in reviews and forums

### Takeaway
Users ask for quick whole-flock egg logging, per-hen detail (egg colour, sorting), CSV export, archiving birds that died or were culled, and offline use with no account and no subscription. Some forum users say a notebook or whiteboard is enough. Subscription pricing causes friction.

### Cited Findings
- FlockFriend reviews: Relyt72 (Mar 14) wants egg colour assigned per chicken. Jes2225 (Jul 3) wants a total bird count and sorting by type or gender. Newgirl88 (Aug 8) has too many hens to log each one and wanted a single daily total, which shipped in v3.7.0. Jo Emanuel (07/11/2025) asked for CSV export and archiving harvested birds, which shipped in v2.1.0 per the developer — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6745474579)
- BackyardChickens forum: Flockstar is the favourite but its pricing is confused (free vs. subscription vs. $30/year). Some users prefer a notebook or whiteboard, and one says most backyard flocks don't need an app — [BYC](https://www.backyardchickens.com/threads/best-apps-for-managing-flock.1335556); [BYC poll](https://www.backyardchickens.com/threads/how-do-you-all-keep-track-of-you-eggs-to-date-poll.1633858/page-2)
- A BYC thread specifically asks for inexpensive options — [BYC](https://www.backyardchickens.com/threads/best-chicken-and-egg-tracking-app.1685418) (thread returned 403; content known only from a search snippet)
- Developers on BYC are still building new egg trackers and recruiting beta testers, so demand is recurring — [BYC](https://www.backyardchickens.com/threads/i-built-a-little-egg-flock-tracking-app-looking-for-beta-testers.1683735)
- Listings use "offline, no account" and "one-time purchase, no subscription" as selling points (Backyarder, Roost, HenWise, CotCot), which suggests users push back on subscriptions — [Backyarder](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6755912571); [Roost](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/roost-backyard-chickens/id6761755516); [CotCot](https://mwm.ai/apps/cotcot-mon-poulailler/6778824979)
- Easy Poultry Manager's 3.97★ average from 304 ratings is middling. Individual reviews were not visible — [appgoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/com.zaheer.poultry)

### Inferences
- Low-friction logging (a single daily flock total) matters more than per-bird detail for most keepers. An app that keeps reminders few and actionable fits the "a notebook is enough" crowd better.

### Gaps
- I could not access Google Play review text for any app, including Arabic ones, so I have no evidence on complaints about language support, reminders not firing, or too many notifications.
- No Reddit r/BackYardChickens or r/chickens threads surfaced in search. Only BackyardChickens.com forum threads did.

## 5. Features for ducks, geese, turkeys, quail, pigeons

### Takeaway
Multi-species support usually means a species dropdown in farm managers (Easy Poultry, EggPulse). Pigeons and ornamental birds are served by separate breeding/pedigree apps, which are popular in Arabic. No species-specific reminder logic (for example quail lighting or duck water needs) was found.

### Cited Findings
- Easy Poultry & Chicken Manager supports chickens, ducks, turkeys, quail, peacocks "and more" — [appgoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/com.zaheer.poultry)
- EggPulse covers turkeys, ducks and geese — [Google Play snippet](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.eggpulse.app)
- "إدارة الطيور" covers poultry, falcons, pigeons, parrots and ornamental birds — [Google Play snippet](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.wwr.birds&hl=en_US)
- مربي (com.morbi.manager), CBSM Breeder (canary) and mBirdBreeder (aviary) focus on pairs, genetics and pedigree for ornamental birds — [mربي](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.morbi.manager&hl=en_US); [CBSM](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.cbsm.breeder&hl=en_US); [mBirdBreeder](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.mbirdbreeder.app&hl=en_US)
- HenWise and CotCot are chicken-only (CotCot has 29 chicken breed profiles) — [HenWise](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/henwise/id6762583691); [CotCot](https://mwm.ai/apps/cotcot-mon-poulailler/6778824979)

### Inferences
- An app that gives each species its own reminders and care data (ducks, geese, quail, pigeons, rabbits) would stand out, since competitors treat species as a label.

### Gaps
- No dedicated quail or pigeon husbandry-reminder app was examined in depth.
